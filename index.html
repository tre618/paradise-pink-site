Our Story

Built in Honor of DeGeta Cole

Paradise Pink is an all-pink music and culture festival built with purpose.

Created by D'Antré Cole in honor of his mother, DeGeta Cole, Paradise Pink was born from love, loss, and the desire to transform heartbreak into hope. Pink was DeGeta's favorite color, and it lives at the heart of everything Paradise Pink represents.

DeGeta and her husband, Rodney Cole, were incredible parents who believed in giving, loving, and serving others. Their home was always open — not only to their own children, but to other kids who needed guidance, support, or simply a place where they felt loved. They raised their family with compassion, generosity, and a deep sense of community, values that continue to inspire everything Paradise Pink stands for today.

After bravely battling breast cancer, DeGeta contracted COVID-19 while undergoing chemotherapy. Even through her treatments, she remained hopeful and focused on getting healthy because she and Rodney had planned the trip of a lifetime to Paris in 2021. Tragically, that trip never came. DeGeta passed away on December 31, 2020, leaving behind a family that was completely devastated.

Just weeks earlier, she had celebrated her birthday surrounded by D'Antré and their family. None of them realized it would be the last birthday they would ever share together.

DeGeta 1955 – 2020

Determined to honor his mother's legacy, D'Antré poured his grief into creation instead of giving up. In just four months, he released six films while building an ecosystem of companies, creative projects, events, and communities designed to bring people together. Of everything he has created, Paradise Pink is the most personal. It is his greatest tribute to the woman who taught him the importance of love, kindness, and making people feel like they belong.

Paradise Pink brings together music, community, fashion, culture, and impact for one unforgettable experience. Every guest is invited to wear pink — not simply as a color, but as a symbol of unity, remembrance, strength, hope, and love.

In partnership with a major breast cancer organization, a portion of every ticket sold directly supports the fight against breast cancer. Every ticket, every sponsor, every performance, and every shared moment helps move that mission forward.

This festival is for the survivors. For the families. For the people we've lost. For those still fighting. And for everyone searching for a place where they are welcomed, celebrated, and never alone.

Paradise Pink is more than a festival. It is a movement — built from love, powered by community, and created to honor DeGeta Cole's legacy while bringing hope to families for generations to come.

The Vision

A Festival Built to Grow

Paradise Pink is designed from day one as a scalable, annual event property — not a single show. Starting with an estimated 1,500–3,000 person debut in Los Angeles in October 2027, the long-term vision is a touring festival series that carries music, culture, and cause into cities across the country.

Our Mission

Paradise Pink Festival is a purpose-driven music and culture festival founded by D'Antré Cole in honor of his mother, DeGeta Cole. Inspired by her love, strength, and legacy, Paradise Pink was created to bring people together through world-class music, fashion, wellness, and community while raising awareness for breast cancer. As a lifelong music lover with a passion for discovering incredible talent across pop, R&B, house, reggae, rock, hip-hop, Latin, Afrobeats, country, and more, D'Antré has spent years introducing people to thousands of artists they may have never discovered otherwise — even during his years driving rideshare, where passengers constantly asked about the music he played. Paradise Pink exists to give talented emerging artists from around the world a larger stage to connect with new audiences alongside established performers. More than a festival, it's a movement built on purpose, belonging, positivity, and unforgettable experiences, with a vision to grow into a multi-city celebration that spreads breast cancer awareness in every city it visits, uplifts communities, creates opportunities, and leaves a lasting impact for generations to come.

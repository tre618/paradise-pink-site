Our Mission

Community, Culture & Awareness

Paradise Pink is a growing community brand built around one idea: awareness works best when people feel welcome. We create experiences where music, wellness, and connection make learning about breast health feel like celebration instead of fear.

Community Events

Socials and gatherings that bring people together in pink, with a cause at the center.

Breast Cancer Education

Clear, trustworthy information on screening, symptoms, risk, and where to find support.

Music & Culture

Live music, DJs, comedy, and creative collaborations that reflect the communities we serve.

Wellness Experiences

Fitness, self-care, and wellness moments that celebrate living well.

Storytelling

Survivor and family stories, including DeGeta Cole's, told with honesty and care.

Strategic Partnerships

Brands, venues, and organizations helping us reach more people and grow the movement.

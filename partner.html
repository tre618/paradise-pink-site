Founder

D'Antré Cole is a filmmaker and creative entrepreneur who founded Paradise Pink in honor of his mother. He leads the brand's vision, creative direction, and strategic partnerships.

Mission

Paradise Pink is a music, culture, and events brand that brings people together to celebrate life, build community, and spread breast cancer awareness.

DeGeta Cole's Story

DeGeta Cole was a Stage 1 breast cancer survivor. After additional chemotherapy intended to help reduce the risk of recurrence, she contracted COVID-19 and passed away in 2020. Her legacy is the heart of Paradise Pink.

October 18 Event Recap

Paradise Pink's first event, an Ice Cream & Dessert Social during Breast Cancer Awareness Month, welcomed 155 guests. HAHAs for TATAs attended and ran a raffle for their November 7 comedy show, a collaboration supported by Paradise Pink.
